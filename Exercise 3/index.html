Frequently Asked Questions

Clarity on energy efficiency terms and practices.

The Energy Rating Label provides a star rating and an annual energy consumption number to help you compare the efficiency of different appliances. It is a mandatory requirement for many electrical products sold in Australia, regulated by the GEMS (Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards) act.

Beyond choosing high-star rated appliances, you can save by: turning appliances off at the wall to avoid standby power, running washing machines or dishwashers only when full, and adjusting thermostat temperatures by a few degrees.

Efficient appliances require less electricity to perform the same task. This not only lowers your ongoing utility bills but also reduces the overall demand on the power grid, leading to fewer greenhouse gas emissions and a smaller carbon footprint for your household.
